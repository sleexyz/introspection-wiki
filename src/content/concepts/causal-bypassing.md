The term comes from [Morris & Plunkett (2025)](/papers/morris2025-causal-bypassing), who argue that tests of LLM introspection need to rule it out. This page is a placeholder until that post has a full summary here.

[Atkinson et al. (2026)](/papers/atkinson2026-identifying-introspection) cite it for the point that noticing a model's testimony matches its behavior is not enough to establish [grounding](/concepts/grounding): the report has to come from the true cause.
